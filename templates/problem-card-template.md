# Example Problem

## Problem

State the problem in one paragraph. A problem is a question with a checkable
answer, not the name of an artefact that should exist.

## Why It Matters

Explain who is misled or blocked while the problem stays open.

## Scope

Define what is in and out of scope, including geography, products, and time
resolution.

## Definition Of Done

List the artefacts and tests that would move the status to
`REFERENCE_AVAILABLE` or `SOLVED_FOR_SCOPE`. Each item should be something an
outsider could verify.

## Baseline

Name the naive alternative that any proposed reference must beat or replace.

## Existing Artefacts

List known references without overstating what they show. Say why each falls
short of the definition of done.

## Open Questions

- What remains unresolved?

  guardrails live in `MARKET_INTEGRITY_POLICY.md` and
  `DATA_AND_LICENSE_POLICY.md`.

# Modo Energy ME BESS DE Benchmark

## Summary

Commercial German battery revenue benchmark launched on 20 May 2026. It
simulates a representative 1h, 2h, or 4h battery cross-optimised across
German markets on real prices, because public asset-level operational data do
not exist in Germany. The launch article states that an 80% calibration
factor brings simulated revenues into line with realised performance, as
validated against GB where both virtual and real-asset data are available.

## Fit To Problem

Evidence that a systematic, regularly published German series exists. It is
not transparent, because the dispatch logic is not public, and it has not
been shown to be previsible, so it does not satisfy `EP-004`. Useful as a
comparator and as a marker of the gap.

## Caveats

- Commercial product; series and methodology detail sit behind subscription.
- Not open data and not reproducible from public material.
- The same provider's GB index is regulated as a benchmark; this card makes
  no statement about the governance of the German product.
